Architectural DNA

Classical Precision

Greek architecture is built on ratio, symmetry, and load carried through proportion rather than mass — the column doing exactly the work it needs to and no more. That same discipline governs how we size a chassis, place a sensor mount, or balance a counterweight.

Where Gothic-Modern gives us verticality and exposed structure, Classical gives us restraint: clean lines, calculated proportion, and nothing added without purpose.

Proportion

Every load-bearing element sized to its function — the same logic behind our chassis and mounting geometry.

Symmetry

Balanced forms that read as stable at a glance — informing our control-cabinet and enclosure layouts.

Restraint

Clean stone surfaces with no ornament that isn't structural — echoed in our unornamented steel and glass palette.
