Field Services

Electro Installation

Wiring is the part of a building nobody sees once it is handed over — which is exactly why it should not be done piecemeal. We design, install, and sign off the whole chain: from the supply and the board down to the last socket.

Every job starts with a load calculation, not a materials list. Once it is clear how much power the building actually needs and where it will be drawn, everything else — cable sizes, protection, the number of circuits — follows from that.

How it works

From calculation to handover

01

Site visit and load calculation

The existing supply, the planned loads, and whatever will be connected later. Free, with no obligation to continue.

02

Schematic and quote

Circuit split, board layout, and cable routes, at a fixed price and a fixed date.

03

Installation

Routes, board, protection, and connections. We clear the site at the end of every day.

04

Testing and handover

Insulation and earthing measurements, schematics, and certificates. The board is left labelled so it still reads years later.
