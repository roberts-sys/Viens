Core Technology

Autonomous Machinery

We're engineering a full sensor stack for excavators and dozers — LiDAR, ultrasonic rangefinders, encoders, accelerometers, and gyroscopes — unified under a central AI control system now in active development. The machine keeps its raw hydraulic power; we're building the judgment to use it precisely.

Our sensor fusion architecture is being validated in real time on test sites, training a control loop to plan dig paths, hold grade, and stop before contact — the next generation of autonomous decisions, forged in the field, not just the lab.

Sensor Fusion

Central AI Control

A single control system governs dig cycles, grading, and collision avoidance across the fleet.

Built for Retrofit
