2. What data we process

When you fill in the contact form, we receive:

  • Your name — so we know how to address you;
  • Your email address — so we can reply;
  • Your message — so we understand your enquiry.

We do not ask for or collect any other personal data. For visitor statistics and cookies, see section 6.

3. Why we process it, and on what basis

We process this data to answer your enquiry and, where relevant, to prepare a quote. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR — steps taken at your request prior to entering a contract — and Article 6(1)(f) — our legitimate interest in corresponding with clients and enquirers.

4. How long we keep it

Contact form messages are kept for 24 months from the date we receive them, then deleted. If the enquiry leads to a contract, the relevant data is retained for as long as accounting and tax law requires.

5. Who else sees it

We do not sell or rent your data. Only the following service providers, acting on our behalf, have access:

  • Netlify — website hosting and contact form processing. Netlify is a US company, so data may be processed outside the EEA under appropriate safeguards.
  • GoatCounter — visitor statistics. This tool sets no cookies, does not track you across websites, and does not collect personal data. It runs at all times.
  • Google Analytics — more detailed visitor analytics. Runs only if you have consented to it. Google is a US company, so data may be processed outside the EEA under appropriate safeguards.

6. Cookies

This website uses no advertising cookies and sells no data to advertisers. Cookies are set only by Google Analytics, and only if you have explicitly agreed by choosing “Accept” in the consent bar.

If you choose “Decline”, or simply do not respond, Google Analytics is never loaded and its cookies are never set. Your choice is stored in your browser so the bar does not reappear on every visit.

Visitor statistics via GoatCounter run regardless of your choice, because that tool sets no cookies and collects no personal data.

You can withdraw consent at any time: reset my cookie choice. The consent bar will then appear again so you can choose differently.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access your data and receive a copy of it;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • request erasure of your data;
  • restrict or object to processing;
  • receive your data in a portable format.

To exercise any of these, write to ziedinagildecustomersupport@gmail.com. We will respond within one month.

If you believe we are handling your data unlawfully, you may lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority — the Latvian State Data Inspectorate (dvi.gov.lv).

8. Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle data, we will update this page and the date shown above.
